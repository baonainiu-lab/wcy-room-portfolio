## 2 · Sarah Aliriel：海报式首屏

## 3 · LekoArts / Jodie：大图作品墙

[首页](https://jodie.lekoarts.de/) · [源码](https://github.com/LekoArts/gatsby-starter-portfolio-jodie) · [0BSD](https://github.com/LekoArts/gatsby-starter-portfolio-jodie/blob/master/LICENSE)
